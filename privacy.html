How this website handles your enquiry.

The enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message on your device. This website does not submit the form to an academy database. You choose whether to send the message in WhatsApp.

If sent, your name, age, playing level, preferred programme and contact number will be shared with TWK to respond to your enquiry and discuss arrangements. Please do not include sensitive information.

Your language preference may be stored in this browser. External services, including WhatsApp, Google Maps, Instagram and Facebook, have their own privacy practices.

For questions about information you have shared with TWK, contact tanweekiongba@gmail.com.
