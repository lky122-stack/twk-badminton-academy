BUILT AROUND THE GAME.

Founded by Tan Wee Kiong, TWK welcomes beginners, recreational players and those working towards competition in Kuala Lumpur.

FOUNDER / 创办人

TAN WEE KIONG

Representing Malaysia on the international stage, Tan Wee Kiong won Olympic silver and the Denmark Open men’s doubles title with Goh V Shem in 2016. His national team experience includes the Thomas Cup.
